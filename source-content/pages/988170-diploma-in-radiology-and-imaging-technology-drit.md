      title: Scope in Imaging and Radiology
  - [text-editor]
      editor: <p data-start="468" data-end="873">Radiology is the branch of medicine that uses imaging techniques to diagnose and sometimes treat diseases. Imaging helps doctors see inside the body without surgery. Common types of medical imaging include:</p><ol><li>X-rays</li><li>Ultrasound (including echocardiography)</li><li>CT scans (Computed Tomography)</li><li>MRI (Magnetic Resonance Imaging)</li><li>Nuclear medicine scans (e.g., PET scans)</li></ol><h4><b>X-ray</b></h4><p><span style="font-size: 16px;">How it works: Uses electromagnetic radiation to create images of bones and certain tissues. Dense structures like bones absorb more X-rays and appear white, while softer tissues appear gray, and air appears black.</span></p><div><p><strong>Uses:</strong></p><ul><li><span style="font-size: 16px;">Detect bone fractures</span></li><li>Check for infections (like pneumonia)</li><li>Detect tumors</li><li>Evaluate joint abnormalities</li></ul><p data-start="468" data-end="873"><b>Advantages: </b><br />Quick, widely available, inexpensive</p><p data-start="468" data-end="873"><b>Limitations: </b><br />Limited detail for soft tissues, involves small radiation exposure</p></div>
  - [text-editor]
      editor: <p></p> <div> <p data-start="468" data-end="873"><b>&nbsp;</b></p> <p data-start="468" data-end="873"><b>&nbsp;</b></p> <p data-start="468" data-end="873"><b>&nbsp;</b></p> <h4><b>Echocardiography (Echo Scan)</b></h4> <p data-start="468" data-end="873"><b>Type</b><br>Ultrasound imaging specifically for the heart</p> <p data-start="468" data-end="873"><b>How it works: </b><br>Uses high-frequency sound waves to create images of the heart in real time.</p> <p data-start="468" data-end="873"><b>Uses:</b></p> <ul> <li>Evaluate heart structure: chambers, valves, and walls</li> <li>Measure heart function (how well the heart pumps)</li> <li>Detect congenital heart defects or heart disease</li> <li>Assess blood flow with Doppler echocardiography</li> </ul> <p data-start="468" data-end="873"><b>Advantages: </b><br>Non-invasive, no radiation, real-time imaging</p> <p data-start="468" data-end="873"><b>Types of echo:</b></p> <ul> <li>Transthoracic echocardiogram (TTE): <br>Standard external scan</li> <li>Transesophageal echocardiogram (TEE): <br>Probe goes down the esophagus for detailed images</li> <li>Stress echo: <br>Evaluates heart function under stress</li> </ul> </div>
