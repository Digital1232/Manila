  - [text-editor]
      editor: <p>A respiratory lab is a specialized area dedicated to diagnosing and managing lung and breathing disorders. Major conditions evaluated include: Bronchial Asthma, COPD, Interstitial Lung Disease, Bronchiectasis, Post-TB &amp; Post-COVID lung diseases, Pulmonary Hypertension, Lung and Thoracic Cancers, Sleep-disordered Breathing (including Sleep Apnea), and Lung Infections (such as tuberculosis, bacterial, and fungal infections).</p><p>The lab provides a range of advanced diagnostic tests, including:</p><ul><li>Pulmonary Function Testing (PFT) – to diagnose, stage, and monitor lung diseases</li><li>Impulse Oscillometry (IOS)</li><li>FeNO Test Machine – for airway inflammation</li><li>6-Minute Walk Test – for exercise tolerance and lung function</li><li>Spirometry Machine – for measuring airflow and lung capacity</li></ul>
