DNM · 2 Years

Diploma in Nursing & Midwifery (DNM)

This program offers comprehensive training in patient care, nursing fundamentals, and maternity services. Students gain in-depth knowledge of anatomy, pharmacology, maternal health, neonatal care, and community nursing.

Key Skills Covered

  • Basic and advanced nursing procedures
  • Antenatal and postnatal care
  • First aid and emergency response
  • Nutrition and patient hygiene

Career Opportunities

Nursing assistants, midwives, home care providers, and staff nurses in hospitals, maternity clinics, and rural health centers.

Course details Apply for DNM

DMLT · 2 Years

Diploma in Medical Laboratory Technology (DMLT)

This course trains students in clinical laboratory techniques essential for diagnosing diseases. Learners master testing procedures for blood, urine, and tissue samples using modern laboratory equipment.

Key Skills Covered

  • Hematology, microbiology, pathology
  • Biochemistry and serology
  • Handling and processing lab samples
  • Operation of lab instruments

Career Opportunities

Lab technicians in hospitals, diagnostic labs, pathology centres, and research institutions.

Course details Apply for DMLT

DOTT · 2 Years

Diploma in Operation Theatre Technology (DOTT)

DOTT students are trained to assist in pre-operative, intra-operative, and post-operative procedures. The course focuses on sterilization, instrumentation, and surgical support.

Key Skills Covered

  • OT setup and maintenance
  • Sterilization and infection control
  • Assisting in major/minor surgeries
  • Anaesthesia support

Career Opportunities

Operation theatre assistants, surgical technologists, anaesthesia aides in hospitals, trauma centres, and day surgery units.

Course details Apply for DOTT

DRIT · 2 Years

Diploma in Radiology and Imaging Technology (DRIT)

This course equips students to operate medical imaging equipment like X-rays, CT scans, MRI machines, and ultrasound systems. It emphasizes patient safety and image analysis.

Key Skills Covered

  • Radiographic techniques and positioning
  • Equipment calibration and maintenance
  • Radiation safety protocols
  • Anatomy and pathology interpretation through imaging

Career Opportunities

X-ray technicians, CT/MRI operators, radiology assistants in hospitals, diagnostic imaging centres, and mobile radiology units.
