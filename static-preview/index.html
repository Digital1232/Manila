Our legacy

Nivya Respiratory Centre started its journey in 1994

Founded by Dr. P. Manimozhian M.D. (Chest Medicine), DTCD, Dip in Allergy (UK), FCCP (USA).

Not All Heroes Wear Capes — Some Wear White Coats.

Every single day, there are individuals who wake up with one unwavering mission: to heal. They don’t know who they’ll help or the stories behind each face—but they show up with the same compassion, dedication, and heart.

At Nivya Respiratory and Multispeciality Hospital, we are proud to be led by such a hero — Dr. P. Manimozhian, a pioneer in pulmonary care in the southernmost part of India.

Our Legacy Begins: Dr. P. Manimozhian’s Vision

Founded in 1994, Nivya Respiratory Centre was the brainchild of Dr. P. Manimozhian M.D. (Chest Medicine), DTCD, Dip in Allergy (UK), FCCP (USA). With a clear mission to provide dedicated and specialized pulmonary care to the people of Southern India, he laid the foundation for a centre that would later evolve into a benchmark for respiratory medicine.

In May 2002, Dr. Manimozhian furthered his specialization in Allergy care at the National Respiratory Training Centre, Warwick, UK, adding a global perspective to our local healthcare commitment.

A Milestone in Respiratory Health: Nivya Respiratory & Multispeciality Hospital

As demand grew, so did our scope. Dr. Manimozhian expanded the outpatient services and established the Nivya Respiratory and Multispeciality Hospital in Tirunelveli, offering world-class pulmonology services with state-of-the-art infrastructure.

Since 1994

Our History

  1. 1994

    Beginning

    Marked the beginning of the hospital, established under the visionary guidance of Dr. P. Manimozhian.

  2. 2002

    Introduction

    Introduced advanced Allergy Specialisation, setting a regional benchmark in immunology care.

  3. 2006

    Hospital

    First hospital in Tirunelveli and South Districts to establish a dedicated Department of Pulmonology, transforming respiratory care in the region.

  4. 2013

    PCR – TB Testing

    Introduced PCR-based TB Testing — delivering results in 1 hour and 30 minutes, enabling faster TB diagnosis and treatment.

  5. 2018

    Pulmonology

    Pulmonology department graduates trained experts into mainstream clinical roles.

  6. 2023

    Dr. Nivya

    Welcomed Dr. Nivya into the leadership team, strengthening hospital operations and academic collaborations.

  7. 2025

    Finding TB

    Launch of “Find TB Before It Spreads” initiative – an advanced screening test that helps detect latent tuberculosis before it affects major organs.
